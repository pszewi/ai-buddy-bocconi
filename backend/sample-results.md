## Q02
Question: List the steps an international student must follow to register with Italy's National Health Service (SSN) in Milan.
Latency: 11.874s
Status: 200
Verticale: relocation
Answer:
The steps an international student must follow to register with Italy's National Health Service (SSN) in Milan are:

1. **Eligibility**: Must have a valid permit of stay to apply for SSN services through the local ASL (or ATS) office in Milan.

2. **Go to the "Scelta e Revoca" office** at the Milan ATS health office to register.

3. **Bring the required documents**:
   - Valid identity document (ID/passport).
   - Fiscal code (codice fiscale).
   - Self-certification of residence/domicile.
   - Previous doctor withdrawal certificate (if registering temporarily from another ASL/ATS).
   - Appropriate documents validating your status (student status, permit of stay, etc.).

4. **Choose a general practitioner (medico curante)** from those available in the Milan area at the time of registration.

5. **Receive a provisional paper health card** immediately upon registration.

6. **Wait for the Carta Regionale dei Servizi (CRS)**, an electronic smart card that will be delivered to your residence address within about one month.

7. **Temporary registration (valid up to 1 year, renewable)** is available for students who temporarily live outside their place of ASL/ATS of residence.

For more details and to confirm procedures, students can consult the ATS Milano website or call +39 02.85.781.

These instructions apply mainly to non-EU international students registered at Bocconi or living in Milan and holding valid residence permits.

## Q06
Question: For the Bocconi MSc graduate Exchange Program selection score, how are academic GPA, credits, and Bachelor degree grade weighted? Show the weights and explain.
Latency: 8.097s
Status: 200
Verticale: study_abroad
Answer:
For the Bocconi MSc graduate Exchange Program selection score, the weighting is as follows:

- Academic weighted credit average (GPA): 50%
- Number of credits earned divided by the maximum number of credits that can be earned: 20%
- Final Bachelor degree grade (Laurea Triennale): 30%

The score formula combines these weights and is then multiplied by 1000 to get the final score.
