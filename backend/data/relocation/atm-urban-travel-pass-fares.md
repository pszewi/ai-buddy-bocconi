# ATM urban travel pass fares for Milan

ATM urban passes cover travel within the Municipality of Milan on ATM urban transport and the city sections of Trenord and S lines.

For Milan urban passes:

- Young people under 27 can buy an annual urban pass for **EUR 200**.
- Young people under 27 can buy a monthly urban pass for **EUR 22**.
- The ordinary adult annual urban pass costs **EUR 330**.
- The ordinary adult monthly urban pass costs **EUR 39**.

The under-27 annual urban pass is therefore **EUR 130 cheaper** than the ordinary annual urban pass. Relative to the ordinary EUR 330 annual fare, the under-27 EUR 200 annual fare is about **39% cheaper**.
