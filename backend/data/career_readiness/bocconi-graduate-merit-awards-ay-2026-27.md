# Bocconi Graduate Merit Awards a.y. 2026-27

The Bocconi Graduate Merit Award for first-year MSc applicants in a.y. 2026-27 is a **100% waiver on Bocconi ordinary academic tuition and fees**.

The award format is a **full tuition and fees waiver**. The page does not describe the standard Graduate Merit Award as a cash stipend.

Key rules:

- Merit Awards are for first-year Graduate applicants for the 2026-2027 academic year.
- Candidates are considered based on the admission application submitted to Bocconi; a separate application is not required.
- The award is based primarily on academic achievement, with the full admissions dossier also evaluated.
- Award assignment is communicated together with admission results.
- Awarded students must complete the online scholarship acceptance procedure within the enrollment deadline.
- The award can be renewed for the legal duration of the regular course of study if enrollment and merit requirements are met.
- For second-year renewal, the page states that awardees must earn at least 50 credits by 10 September 2027.
