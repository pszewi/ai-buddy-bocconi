# Career fairs | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps students understand the types of employer events Bocconi runs and the sectors/geographies covered.

## Key Facts

- Bocconi career fairs let employers connect with students and graduates, showcase internship and job offers, interact online or in presence, and collect applications.
- The 2025 wrapped figures listed: 2 editions of Bocconi&Jobs with 285 participating employers and 6,200+ participating students and graduates; 7 career fairs for specific business fields with 300 participating employers and 4,200+ participating students and graduates; and 7 editions of Bocconi&Jobs abroad with 160+ participating employers and 1,300+ participating students and graduates.
- Bocconi&Jobs is the main career fair, with Spring and Fall editions open to employers from all business fields and countries.
- Bocconi&Jobs abroad is dedicated to specific geographical areas including Benelux, France, Germany and Switzerland, Luxembourg, Middle East, Spain and Portugal, and UK and Ireland.
- Investment Banking Days is an orientation and recruiting event for Investment Banking, traditionally held at the beginning of the fall semester.
- Recruiting Dates are organized for Arts, Sport & Entertainment; Banking, Financial Services & Fintech; Fashion, Luxury & Beauty; Government, Public Services & International Organizations; and Web & IT Companies.
