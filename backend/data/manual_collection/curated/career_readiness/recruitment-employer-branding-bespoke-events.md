# Recruitment and employer branding bespoke events | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps answer what bespoke recruiting/employer-branding opportunities students may encounter through Bocconi employer partnerships.

## Key Facts

- Bocconi organizes targeted recruiting and employer branding initiatives with employers interested in hiring and developing young professionals.
- Within the Corporate Associate Program framework, companies and partner law firms can create early engagement with students, source profiles for internship and job offers, and collaborate on professional orientation.
- Formats listed include company presentations, networking events, company visits, and one-on-one meetings with students.
- Corporate Associates and Partner Law Firms can use advanced JobGate features to post internship and job offers, manage applications, and access the CV Book.
- Corporate Associates and Partner Law Firms have priority access to events and initiatives such as Bocconi&Jobs and In-Company Trainings.
