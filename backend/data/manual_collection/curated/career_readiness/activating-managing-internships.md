# Activating and managing internships | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps answer how Bocconi internships are activated and what documentation/timing constraints employers and students must follow.

## Key Facts

- Internships in Italy and abroad are activated through an Internship Agreement between the employer and Bocconi University.
- The Internship Agreement is a general agreement that must be signed only once by the employer and includes rights and duties of the parties in the internship relationship.
- The agreement must be finalized before the student starts the internship, even for unpaid experiences.
- For internships in Italy, the Internship Agreement procedure must be completed through JobGate.
- For internships outside Italy, employers fill in the Internship Agreement form from JobGate and upload the signed copy in JobGate.
- The page says employers have responsibilities such as providing an educational project and appointing a company tutor.

## Contacts, Locations, And Portals

- The page points employers to JobGate for internship activation procedures.
