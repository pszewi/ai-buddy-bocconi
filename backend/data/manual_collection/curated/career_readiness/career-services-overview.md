# Career Services | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps students understand Bocconi career support, job/internship portals, employer access, and graduate outcomes.

## Key Facts

- Career Services support students and graduates entering the job market through recruiting, networking and career counseling activities during the academic year.
- Available services listed include Career Guidance & Approaching the Job Market, Meet with employers, JobGate for applying online, and internship support from start to credit recognition.
- The page reports 94.5% of MSc graduates employed 1 year after graduation and 36.2% employed abroad.
- For 2025, the page reports 16 career fairs in Italy and abroad with 700+ participating employers, 740+ employers involved in meetings with students and graduates, and 9,300+ internship and job opportunities posted online.
