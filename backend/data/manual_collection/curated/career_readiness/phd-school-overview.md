# PhD | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps answer questions about Bocconi PhD programs as a research-career path, including financing and job-market support.

## Key Facts

- The PhD School coordinates Bocconi PhD programs and acts as a point of reference for PhD candidates.
- PhD programs listed: Business Administration and Management; Economics and Finance; Legal Studies; Mathematics and Applications; Social and Political Science; Statistics and Computer Science.
- The page states that PhD candidates can advance knowledge and conduct research with Bocconi Faculty and through exchanges with leading American and European universities.
- The Invernizzi PhD Financial Aid supports PhD candidates during the year before thesis defense, when they focus on writing and presenting research papers to enhance prospects for international academic job-market placements.
- From a.y. 2024-25, the Invernizzi support also extends to first-year PhD students; the page says the Ospitalità PORTUS program gives first-year PhD students a scholarship higher than the standard amount for other years to better cope with Milan relocation costs.
- Starting from a.y. 2025-26, Tech Europe Foundation supports Bocconi PhD programs through full funding of 8 fellowships, expected to grow in coming years.
