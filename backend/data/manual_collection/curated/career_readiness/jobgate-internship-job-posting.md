# JobGate: internship and job posting | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps students and employers understand JobGate as Bocconi’s job/internship portal and related recruiting tools.

## Key Facts

- JobGate is Bocconi’s portal for posting internship and job opportunities and managing internship activation.
- Internship announcements may target students from all study years and may also be aimed at recent graduates within 12 months after graduation for extracurricular internships.
- Job announcements can be dedicated to junior profiles requiring up to 3 years of experience and senior profiles requiring more than 3 years of experience.
- Use of JobGate is free, and companies can post as many offers as they wish.
- Exception: Head Hunting Agencies can publish job offers for themselves and client companies, and internship offers for themselves only, with a limit of 12 offers per year and maximum 2 offers simultaneously.
- The CVBook Directory is the online database of CVs of Bocconi University graduates and is accessed from JobGate.
- The DAL Service - Disabilities and Work supports companies in identifying and hiring Bocconi students and graduates belonging to Protected Categories.
