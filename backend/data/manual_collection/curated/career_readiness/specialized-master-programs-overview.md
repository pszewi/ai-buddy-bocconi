# Specialized Master Programs | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps answer career-readiness questions about specialized masters, practical training, internships, and program structure.

## Key Facts

- For academic year 2025-2026, Bocconi listed 6 Specialized Master programs: 2 in economics and management, one held in English, and 4 in law, three held in English.
- Pre-experience Specialized Master programs focus on specific topics, company functions, market segments, or areas of expertise and are designed for students who graduated from an undergraduate or graduate program, preferably within the last 2 years.
- Main features: programs last one academic year, with between 400 and 500 teaching hours plus an internship.
- Programs award between 60 and 72 educational credits, with each credit corresponding to 25 hours of student work.
- Students are required to attend at least 80% of the total duration and pass one or more exams assessing acquired skills.
- Admission follows a selection process based on the applicant’s CV, a test and/or an individual interview.
