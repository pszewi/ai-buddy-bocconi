# Inclusion, Disabilities and Learning Disorders | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps students find inclusion, accessibility, disability/SLD/ADHD support, anti-discrimination support, and multifaith resources.

## Key Facts

- The Inclusion Service is a point of reference that collaborates with other offices to guarantee the same opportunities to everyone in the Bocconi community.
- Support areas listed include students with disabilities, SLDs, ADHD and other specific needs; Anti-Discrimination Service Desk; multiculturalism; gender diversity; Bocconi Carriera Alias; and all-gender restrooms.
- The page says there are more than 100 student associations on campus, including associations representing international communities.
- The page says halal, kosher and vegan meals are offered in dining areas to support dietary needs related to religious, ethical and/or cultural beliefs.

## Contacts, Locations, And Portals

- The Multifaith Space entrance is at Piazza Sraffa 6, door on the left, first floor.
- The Multifaith Space is open Monday through Saturday 7:30am-10pm, and Sunday 9am-10pm.
- For general information, students are directed to yoU@B > B in Touch > Inclusion, Disabilities and Wellbeing.
