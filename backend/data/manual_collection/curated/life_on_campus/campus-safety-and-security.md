# On-Campus Safety and Security | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps students know what to do in emergencies and where campus health/safety resources are located.

## Key Facts

- Bocconi defines an emergency as any deviation from normal conditions that may result in potential or actual harm to people, the environment, or property.
- Examples listed include injuries, accidents, fires, explosions, intrusions, thefts, structural collapses, earthquakes, floods, and other extreme weather events.
- Anyone who identifies a dangerous situation or emergency on campus must immediately report it by calling 02 5836 2121.
- The emergency number should also be used to report abnormal situations such as materials left in inappropriate locations, suspicious individuals, or unattended packages/items.
- Emergency management includes building emergency plans, training and drills, regular evacuation drills, procedures for different scenarios, incident assessment, and activation of external emergency services.
- Emergency teams include First Aid Officers, Fire Prevention Officers, Evacuation Officers, and AED Operators.

## Contacts, Locations, And Portals

- Campus emergency number: 02 5836 2121.
- Infirmary location: Residence at Via Bocconi 12.
- On-campus medical center location: Piazza Sraffa 11 building.
