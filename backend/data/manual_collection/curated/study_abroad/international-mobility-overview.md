# International Mobility | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps students identify Bocconi international mobility options and support resources.

## Key Facts

- Bocconi states that its network includes 293 Partner Schools in 56 countries.
- Mobility options listed include Exchange Program for 1 semester abroad, Double Degree for 1 academic year abroad, Network Programs from online courses to 1 semester abroad, and Free Mover from 2 weeks to 1 semester abroad.
- Erasmus+ scholarships are available for selected students for Erasmus+ destinations according to the relevant procedure.
- Scholarships and other forms of financial aid are available to specific categories of students enrolled in a Bocconi program.
- Bocconi has partnered with International SOS to offer medical, safety and security advice to travelling students.
