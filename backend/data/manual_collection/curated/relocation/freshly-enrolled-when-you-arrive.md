# Freshly enrolled students - When you arrive | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps newly arrived international students complete immigration steps and access campus support.

## Key Facts

- For non-EU students, the visa is required for entry into Italy and the Schengen area, but once in Italy they must apply for a Permit of Stay for Study Reasons to stay legally.
- The Permit of Stay application must be made within 8 working days of entry into Italy.
- The page points newly arrived students to welcome activities, Campus Life services, Italian Survival Course, fiscal code information, and Bocconi facilities.
- B in Touch can answer questions about the University; students can visit the B in Touch Point on campus or access B in Touch online from the yoU@B student diary.
- Wi-Fi is available throughout campus; students access the “bocconi-studenti” network with ID number and password after approving terms and conditions in yoU@B.

## Contacts, Locations, And Portals

- For Wi-Fi problems, suggestions, or clarifications, the page lists helpwifi@unibocconi.it.
- B in Touch is available through the yoU@B student diary and at the B in Touch Point.
