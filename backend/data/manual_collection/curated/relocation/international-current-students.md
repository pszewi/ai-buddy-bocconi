# Current Students | Bocconi University

## Why This Page Is Relevant

- Helps international students keep immigration status updated and understand work/internship constraints while studying in Italy.

## Key Facts

- Non-EU students must keep immigration status updated, and the Permit of Stay must be renewed at least 60 days before it expires.
- Before renewing a Permit of Stay, students must request an Official Academic Transcript for immigration purposes certifying at least one exam passed for the first renewal and at least two exams for each subsequent renewal, except for circumstances beyond one’s control.
- The transcript can be requested through cert@B in the yoU@B Diary > Certificates, and the page says the process usually takes 4 working days.
- Non-Italian citizens with a residence permit for study may work, but subordinate work must not exceed 20 hours per week, cumulated over 52 weeks, with an annual limit of 1,040 hours.
- The page states that any work contract cannot extend beyond the validity of the student residence permit.

## Deadlines, Fees, Or Dates

- Permit of Stay renewal: at least 60 days before expiry.
- Official Academic Transcript processing: usually 4 working days according to the source page.
