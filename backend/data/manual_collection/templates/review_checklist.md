# Manual Page Review Checklist

Use this checklist for each record in `backend/data/manual_collection/raw/pages.jsonl`.

## Identify

- Source URL:
- Fetched at:
- Candidate title:
- Primary vertical: `relocation` / `life_on_campus` / `study_abroad` / `career_readiness`
- Duplicate of:

## Relevance

- The page answers a likely Bocconi student question.
- The page has at least two concrete facts after cleanup.
- The page fits the selected vertical rubric.
- The page is not only navigation, marketing, event promotion, or image content.
- The page is not superseded by a newer candidate.

## Grounding

- Requirements are explicitly sourced.
- Deadlines and dates are explicitly sourced.
- Fees and costs are explicitly sourced.
- Contacts are official public office/service contacts.
- Stale or uncertain facts are labeled.
- No facts were inferred or invented.

## Decision

- Decision: `accept` / `reject`
- Confidence: `high` / `medium` / `low`
- Rejection reason if rejected:
- Notes for curated markdown if accepted:
