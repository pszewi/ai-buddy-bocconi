## AG038
Question: Compare the one-semester on-campus dorm cost with six months in a private single room at the estimated low and high monthly prices.
Expected verticale: study_abroad
Returned verticale: relocation
Category: computational
Latency: 10.16s
Status: 200

Expected facts:
On-campus dorm for one semester is EUR 4,000-4,800. A private single room for six months is EUR 3,600 at EUR 600/month to EUR 6,600 at EUR 1,100/month. Thus the dorm range is EUR 400 above the low private estimate but EUR 1,800 below the high private estimate.

Answer:
From the source:

- **On-campus dorm (one semester):** **€4,000–€4,800**
- **Private single room:** **€600–€1,100 per month**

For **six months** in a private single room:

- **Low estimate:** €600 × 6 = **€3,600**
- **High estimate:** €1,100 × 6 = **€6,600**

### Comparison
- Compared with the **low private-room estimate (€3,600)**, the **one-semester on-campus dorm costs €400–€1,200 more**.
- Compared with the **high private-room estimate (€6,600)**, the **one-semester on-campus dorm costs €1,800–€2,600 less**.

So, the dorm semester cost sits **between** the six-month low and high private single-room estimates.
