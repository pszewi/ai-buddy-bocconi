Answer:
According to the available sources, the steps supported for registering with Italy’s National Health Service in Milan are:

1. **Make sure you are eligible**
   - Bocconi states that **only foreign citizens with a valid permit of stay may apply** for the service offered by the ASL/SSN.

2. **Go to the “Scelta e Revoca” office / counter in Milan**
   - The registration is done through the **“Scelta e Revoca”** offices/counter at **ATS Milano**.

3. **Bring the required documents**
   The excerpts list these documents for SSR/SSN registration:
   - **Fiscal code**
   - **Self-certification of residence**
   - **Any health card from the competent health authority**, if applicable
   - **Identity document**
   - If someone goes on your behalf: **delegation** plus the interested person’s ID

4. **Choose your general practitioner**
   - The ASST Milano source says that **registration is mandatory and includes choosing a family doctor**.

5. **Receive temporary proof of registration**
   - At registration, you are issued a **temporary paper card**.
   - Later, usually **within about one month**, the **Carta Regionale dei Servizi** is sent to your residence address.
   - If the registration is **shorter than six months**, this card is **not issued**.

For **EU/EEA/Swiss students staying longer than 3 months**, Bocconi adds a more specific procedure:
1. Obtain the **S1 form** from your home country’s health insurance authority.
2. Go to the **“Scelta e Revoca” counter at ATS Milano**.
3. Bring:
   - a **photocopy of a valid ID**
   - **fiscal code**
   - the **completed S1 form**

Question: For the Bocconi MSc graduate Exchange Program selection score, how are academic GPA, credits, and Bachelor degree grade weighted? Show the weights and explain.
Expected verticale: study_abroad
Returned verticale: study_abroad
Category: computational
Latency: 8.664s
Status: 200

Expected facts:
The Bocconi MSc Exchange Program selection score uses a weighted formula combining: MSc academic GPA (the dominant component, typically ~70%), credits earned at the time of application (~15%), and the Bachelor degree final grade (~15%). GPA is the dominant component; the official figures are specified on the Exchange Program selection page.

Answer:
For the **Bocconi MSc Exchange Program selection score**, the weights are:

- **Academic weighted credit average (WA): 50%**
- **Number of credits earned (NC), divided by the maximum number of credits that can be earned (MAX NC): 20%**
- **Bachelor degree final grade (Laurea Triennale): 30%**

This is explicitly stated in the graduate selection criteria source.

### Explanation
The selection is based on **merit** and combines three elements:

1. **Academic GPA / weighted average (WA) — 50%**  
   This is the most important component. It refers to the student’s **academic weighted credit average**.

2. **Credits — 20%**  
   This part uses the **number of credits earned**, but not as a raw total alone: it is calculated as  
   **NC / MAX NC**,  
   meaning your earned credits are compared with the maximum credits that could have been earned.

3. **Bachelor degree grade — 30%**  
   The **final Bachelor’s degree grade** counts for nearly one-third of the score.

### Additional note from the source
- The result of the formula is **multiplied by 1000** to obtain the **final score**.
- For the **2026–27 selection**, the credits calculation includes exams from the **1st year, 1st semester study plan officially taken by February 3rd, 2026**.
- Exams not part of that study plan, such as **internships, languages, anticipated exams, and anticipated curricular supplementary activities**, are **not included**.
