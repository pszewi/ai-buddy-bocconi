Question: For incoming exchange students in Fall 2026, when is course selection, when is add/drop, how many courses may they select, and can they add language courses?
Expected verticale: study_abroad
Returned verticale: study_abroad

Expected facts:
Course Selection for Fall AY 2026-2027 runs August 24-26, 2026 through yoU@B, first come-first served with no waiting list. Add/Drop is September 7-15, 2026. Exchange students must enroll in minimum 1 and maximum 5 courses per semester. They cannot add language courses to their study plan.

Answer:
For incoming exchange students in Fall 2026 at Bocconi University:

- **Course Selection** period is from **August 24 to August 26, 2026**. Courses are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis with no waiting list.

- **Add/Drop period** is from **September 7 to September 15, 2026**. After this deadline, no changes are allowed on the study plan.

- Students may select **a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 5 courses** per semester.

- Regarding language courses: The Italian Language Crash Course is an optional, extracurricular course (with a registration fee) outside the study plan and does **not** count for credits nor appear on the official transcript. Therefore, language courses cannot be added to the official study plan as credit courses.

In summary, students cannot add language courses as part of their official course selection but can optionally attend Italian Language Crash Courses outside of their formal study plan.
