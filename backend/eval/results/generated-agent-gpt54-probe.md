## AG042
Question: What should an Exchange Program student do before departure regarding passport validity, visa requirements, scholarships, and elective choices?
Expected verticale: study_abroad
Returned verticale: study_abroad
Category: actionable
Latency: 12.385s
Status: 200

Expected facts:
Check immigration policies for destination countries and ensure a valid passport; if the passport expires during or within six months after the program ends, renew it as soon as possible. Provide passport/visa documents when requested after selection. European university selections in Erasmus+ automatically benefit from the scholarship and require documents to the office; other mobilities may have need-based scholarships subject to funds. Enroll in the next academic year and choose electives like other students, with the chance to change elective choices after return and academic recognition.

Answer:
Before departure, an Exchange Program student should:

- **Check passport validity and visa rules**
  - Make sure they have a **valid passport**.
  - Check the **immigration policies** of the countries they are applying to.
  - If the passport **expires during the exchange or within six months after the end** of the study abroad program, it should be **renewed as soon as possible**.
  - If a **passport and/or visa** is required, the student will have to provide a **copy of the valid passport immediately after selection** so the necessary documents can be issued.
  - They may need to apply for a **student visa**; they should check the **host university website** and the **consulate or embassy** for updated requirements and **start early**, since the process can take time.

- **Check scholarship status**
  - Students selected for **European universities participating in Erasmus+** **automatically** receive the **Erasmus+ scholarship**.
  - If they are grantees, they must **submit some documents** to the office.
  - For **Erasmus+ destinations**, they will receive specific instructions, including on the **Mobility Agreement** and **Online Learning Agreement**, and they should **meet all deadlines** to receive the scholarship.
  - Other international mobility scholarships may be assigned **based on need and availability of funds**.

- **Handle elective choices**
  - They must **enroll in the next academic year** like other students.
  - They must also **choose elective courses**.
  - Exchange students may enter **tentative elective choices** and can **change them after returning from the exchange**, once the **Academic Recognition procedures** are completed.

Additionally, before departure, they should make sure that **all courses they intend to take abroad are approved on the Recognition Platform**.
